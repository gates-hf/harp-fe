Three metrics · the row ends after three

Cards are a fixed 260px and never stretch: a row of three ends after three and leaves the trailing space empty, which is the behaviour rather than a gap to fill. The left edge carries severity — neutral, warning, critical — and colour marks risk, never reassurance, so a good number stays neutral. Detail belongs in the title; there is no footer line. A card that acts on the page is a <button>, one that navigates is an <a>, and one that does neither is a <div> with no focus ring. Under 1100px the row becomes two flexible columns, under 700px one at 48px tall.
