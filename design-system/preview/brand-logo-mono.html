The rule

Keep clear space of at least 1 unit on all four sides — equal top, bottom, left, and right. No text, imagery, or edges inside the dashed zone.

1 unit = half the lockup height.

white · on dark
Use monochrome when color can't reproduce: faxed orders, embossing, watermarks, single-color print. Never recolor and never crowd — honor the equal 1-unit zone on every side.
